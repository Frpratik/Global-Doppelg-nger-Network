# DOPPEL Product Specification

## 1. Product Mission & Vision
**Doppel** is a consent-based visual twin discovery platform designed to help consenting individuals discover people around the world who look remarkably like them. 

Unlike biometric surveillance or facial recognition lookup engines, Doppel operates exclusively within a closed, opt-in network of voluntary participants.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Consent-First Matching**: Only matches participants who explicitly enroll.
- **Visual Similarity Index**: Evaluates spatial-frequency features and cosine manifold distances.
- **Zero-Knowledge Identity Protection**: Never scrapes open-web imagery, social platforms, or corporate databases.
- **Granular Privacy & Instant Purge**: Instant discovery toggling and permanent biometric vector deletion.

## 3. User Journey
1. **Registration**: Create account and provide explicit dual consent.
2. **Enrollment**: Take a selfie with real-time blur, lighting, and single-face verification.
3. **Encoding**: Deep neural network generates a 512-dimensional normalized embedding vector.
4. **Discovery**: Interactive radar scanner executes Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) cosine search.
5. **Results & Social**: Inspect visual twin cards, view permissible profile attributes, and send connection requests.
6. **Privacy Controls**: Opt out, pause discovery, or permanently purge facial embeddings.
