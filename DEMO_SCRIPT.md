# DOPPEL 3-Minute Live Hackathon Demo Script

## Timeline

- **0:00 – 0:30 (Hook & Problem)**:
  - *"What if your closest visual twin is already here?"*
  - Show the landing page with the animated canvas radar scanner.
  - Explain why Doppel rejects web-scraping surveillance in favor of voluntary, consent-based peer discovery.

- **0:30 – 1:15 (Live Enrollment & Quality Engine)**:
  - Navigate to `/enroll`.
  - Capture selfie via webcam or upload.
  - Show real-time AI quality checklist (sharpness score, lighting, and single-face confirmation).
  - Submit: System aligns landmarks and generates normalized 512-d ArcFace vector.

- **1:15 – 2:00 (Neural Radar Discovery & Twin Results)**:
  - Navigate to `/discover`.
  - Click **Start Neural Search**.
  - Show progressive radar scanning and candidate reveal: Rank #1 twin with 94.2% visual similarity.
  - Inspect match card: view cranial aspect ratio correlation notes and permissible city info.

- **2:00 – 2:30 (Privacy Enforcement & Opt-Out Demo)**:
  - Open `/dashboard` or `/settings`.
  - Toggle **Discovery: Paused**.
  - Re-run search to prove the user immediately drops out of the searchable vector manifold.

- **2:30 – 3:00 (Biometric Purge & Closing Vision)**:
  - Click **Purge Biometrics**: Show irreversible deletion from the vector store.
  - Conclude with the vision of ethical, consent-based visual AI discovery.
